# The Cost of Non-Deterministic AI in Legacy IT

Enterprises run on deterministic systems (SAP, Oracle, legacy mainframes). Introducing non-deterministic AI into these environments without a translation layer creates massive shadow costs.

**The Shadow Costs:**
1. **Endless QA:** Teams spend more time monitoring the AI than doing the actual work.
2. **Compliance Risks:** Unauditable decisions lead to regulatory fines.
3. **Stalled Deployment:** Projects sit in "sandbox mode" for 12 months because IT refuses to give production access.

ROI only happens when AI operates with 100% predictability.
